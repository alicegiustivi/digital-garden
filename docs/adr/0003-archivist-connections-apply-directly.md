# The Archivist's related and authored-by Connections apply directly, marked and undoable

Connections of kind related and authored by that the Archivist finds after a capture are now added at once with AI Origin, shown ✦ (role, model and date on hover), and each can be undone in one click. An undone Connection is remembered and not proposed again unless the owner asks. About 10 are added per Object (one setting, `maxConnections` in `ai-config.ts`), skipping pairs already connected. Specific kinds (supports, contradicts, builds on, inspired by), a change of kind on an existing Connection, and Connections in Researcher answers stay Suggestions that wait for approval. The Archivist still never creates Objects, changes types or writes Thoughts without approval, and Private Objects are still never read or connected by the AI.

This reverses the earlier rule that the AI never creates a final Connection without approval (issues #7, #8, #16). The owner reversed it after living with Build 9: approving every Connection was more than they could keep up with, and a pile of pending Suggestions was not being reviewed at all. Connections now work like the Archivist's Tags: applied, marked, undoable. We kept approval for the cases where the AI would be claiming a *meaning* (supports, contradicts, ...) rather than just noticing that two things are about the same thing, and for anything that adds an Object or alters a Thought. The cost: a wrong Connection can sit in the Garden until the owner notices the ✦ mark; ADR 0001 keeps it permanently identifiable as AI's, and undo removes it cleanly. When this shipped, the Connections still waiting as related / authored-by Suggestions were applied once (owner's choice), each with undo.
